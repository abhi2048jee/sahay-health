Healthcare shouldn't depend
on your pin code.

SAHAY is an AI health companion that runs on any phone — smart or basic — and helps someone in a village reach the right professional help, in their own language, without needing to know which hospital to call.

The problem

In much of rural India, the nearest qualified help is far away, and the phone in someone's pocket is more likely to be a ₹1,500 keypad device than a smartphone with fast data. SAHAY is built for that phone first, not as an afterthought.

What it isn't

Not a robot doctor. SAHAY never diagnoses or prescribes — it listens, flags urgency, and routes people to a real clinician faster than they'd otherwise get there.
