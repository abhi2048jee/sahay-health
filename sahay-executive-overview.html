Executive Presentation & Project Overview

SAHAY — Healthcare, Wherever You Are

An AI-guided rural healthcare navigation & triage concept, built for low-end phones and multilingual users across India.

Status: Concept + functional prototype complete Stage: Available for handoff, incubation, or acquisition Built by: Independent developer/creator
01 · Executive Summary

The core value proposition

SAHAY is a lightweight, AI-guided health platform that gives anyone with a basic phone — not just a smartphone — a way to describe what's wrong, understand how urgent it is, and get routed to the nearest facility actually equipped to help them, in their own language. It closes the gap between the first moment someone realizes something is wrong and the moment a qualified professional takes over, without ever pretending to be that professional itself. The frontend architecture, localization logic, and full user journey have already been designed and built — what's needed now is a partner to take it from validated prototype to deployed product.

02 · The Problem Space

Why rural healthcare access currently fails

🌍 Geography

The nearest qualified facility is often far away, and the "nearest hospital" on a map isn't always the fastest one to actually reach.

📱 Phone type

Most health tech assumes a smartphone and fast 4G/5G — an assumption that excludes exactly the population that needs this most.

🗣️ Language

Medical terminology and English-only interfaces shut out huge portions of the population from getting clear, actionable guidance.

⏱️ Time-to-triage

Without a fast way to gauge urgency, people either delay seeking help or overwhelm facilities that weren't the right fit in the first place.

03 · Solution Architecture

How it works

🩺 AI Health Assistant & Triage

A conversational voice/text interface that lets users describe symptoms in plain language. The AI evaluates urgency, flags warning signs, and guides the person toward the right level of professional care.

Ethical guardrail: Triage only — SAHAY never diagnoses conditions or prescribes medicine. Every path explicitly ends by connecting the user to a qualified professional.

📶 Low-End Phone & 2G/USSD Accessibility

Runs on basic, affordable feature phones via lightweight web views or USSD modes — zero app-store requirement, no heavy data packages needed.

🌐 Dynamic Multilingual Interface

Built-in language switching across Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, and English, by voice or text — designed to extend to further regional languages.

🗺️ Smart Navigation

Calculates the fastest practical route rather than the closest point on a map — accounting for real-world conditions like seasonal road blocks or facility specialization, so patients reach somewhere actually equipped to help.

🚨 Emergency SOS & Clinician Handoff

High-visibility emergency triggers with instant location sharing, paired with a clean digital intake summary so doctors understand symptoms and urgency the moment the patient arrives.
